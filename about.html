Fast Food Trivia

When did fast food apear in the United State?

British favorite fish and chips appeared in the 19th century with the development of trawler fishing, and the first fish and chips shop opened in 1860 at Tommyfield Market in Oldham. Max Sielaff in Berlin invented automats, vending machine restaurants, in 1896. In 1902, Joseph Horn and James Hardart opened an automat in New York City, marking the beginning of fast food in the United States. (Source: History of Fast Food)
